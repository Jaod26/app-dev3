# Can't Buy Me Love
> Can't Buy Me Love follows a hardworking young man who becomes entangled in a dangerous plot against a wealthy woman, saving her at great personal cost and forging a debt that ties them together. With strong chemistry between leads Donny Pangilinan and Belle Mariano, the show blends romance, mystery, and family intrigue, earning praise for its engaging plot and high production values.

**Main Characters**
- Donny Pangilinan as Bingo
- Belle Mariano as Caroline
- Rhen Escaño as Bingo's adoptive grandmother
- Albie Casiño as Wilson Tiu
